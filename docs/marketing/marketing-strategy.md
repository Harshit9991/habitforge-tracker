# HabitForge - Marketing Strategy (ICP, channels, gates)

REVISED strategy, evidence-tightened. Replaces my previous report. Sources: marketingskills library (installed and verified, commit f7e3adb) for method; product facts from the Oct 22-24 audit; everything predictive is labeled scenario/assumption, and unknowns are stated rather than filled.

=== FEASIBILITY: UNKNOWN, NOT IMPOSSIBLE ===
Correcting my earlier framing: we have not established Harshit's audience size, group memberships, distribution partners or budget, so I cannot rule 1,000 paying users in or out. What I can show is the reach the goal requires, so he can judge it against his actual assets:
- 1,000 buyers at an assumed 1-2% visitor-to-paid conversion implies roughly 50,000-100,000 site visitors in 10 days. At an assumed 5-10% warm-audience conversion it implies 10,000-20,000 warm-reachable people. These conversion figures are scenario assumptions only - the product has no analytics, so no real rate exists yet, and I am not forecasting any outcome.
- The deciding evidence is his reachable audience: which WhatsApp/Telegram/Discord groups he is actually in, their sizes, their self-promotion rules, any friend or creator with distribution, and any budget. We have none of that on record. That is question set 1 below.
- Paid acquisition: viability untested, not ruled out. CAC is unknown and, with no conversion measurement, unmeasurable - buying traffic before measurement exists is unaccountable spend, which is a sequencing point, not an impossibility claim.

=== ICP: INITIAL HYPOTHESIS, NOT A VERDICT ===
Per the customer-research playbook, personas should come from evidence; we have zero first-party data, so this is a proxy-based hypothesis to validate in the first conversations, tagged as such.
Hypothesis: "The deadline-driven resetter" - someone about to start a defined ~90-day push with a hard external deadline (placement season, an exam cycle, a fitness or skill challenge), who has tried habit tracking before and dropped it, and who wants visible proof of progress across the push.
- Narrow trigger: a dated commitment starting in the next few weeks - not "wants to build habits someday." The trigger is what makes a 90-day campaign structure relevant right now.
- Paid-value reason beyond aesthetic: the paid tier sells interpretation of an already-running campaign - patterns across logged habits, pacing vs the success threshold, tips when slipping. The hypothesis is that someone mid-commitment with real stakes pays for evidence that the push is working, and that a one-time payment also acts as a commitment device. Both parts are untested hypotheses, not established value props.
- Age/geography (18-28, students and early-career, India-first) follows from price point and Harshit's likely access, and is itself an assumption to test.
- His campus circle is a lead for first conversations and testimonials, not proven distribution - group access, sizes and promo rules are unknown.

=== CHANNEL RANKING (judgment with unknowns explicit; no verified reach behind any of these) ===
1. Direct outreach from him, in his voice, to people and groups he already belongs to. Highest expected relevance per message; actual reach and group self-promo permission unknown. He sends; we draft.
2. Community posts in self-improvement / habit-tracking communities (Reddit, Discord, Telegram study and productivity groups are candidate types). Fit with the ICP hypothesis is good; but community rules, activity levels and his membership status are unverified, and self-promo is commonly restricted - each target's rules need checking before posting, and outcomes are high-variance.
3. Product Hunt launch. Grounded facts: PH's own launch guide (producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch) says there is no golden weekday; it notes weekend launches get ~15% more Visit-button clicks and suit side projects, and recommends 12:01am PT starts for a full 24-hour cycle (that is ~12:31pm IST). Reach for a first-time founder with no existing audience is unknown; treat as a structured shot plus portfolio evidence, not a forecast.
4. A short screen-recording video of the product for his own social accounts. Cheap asset, reusable across the other channels; reach depends on his existing following, which is unknown to us.
Deliberately not ranked: SEO/content and directories (slow compounding, wrong window), influencer partnerships (negotiation cycles unlikely to close in 10 days), paid ads (sequenced after measurement exists).

=== GATES BEFORE RECRUITING PAYING USERS (his decisions; no work done) ===
1. Data-trust validation first, correcting my earlier "fine to market": cross-device sync and per-user data isolation (RLS) are unverified. Before strangers pay and log personal habits, these should be tested - recruiting paying users onto unverified data handling is the risky order.
2. A known paid-access control weakness undermines both revenue and any paid-conversion measurement; fix before treating paid numbers as real.
3. Measurement for the actual goal: session recordings (Clarity) alone do not establish paid activation. Proving "pay AND use" needs conversion + activation event tracking (signup, purchase, first check-in), whatever tool provides it.
4. Checkout shows Rs 149 on the page but charged Rs 175.82 after GST in the live test - a price-display mismatch that can cause checkout abandonment. Worth deciding the display before a push.
5. No guest mode: forced Google sign-in caps activation. Product decision for him.

=== QUESTIONS THAT DECIDE THE PLAN ===
1. Which groups/communities is he actually in (and roughly how large), and does he know their self-promo rules?
2. Any budget at all, or zero-spend only?
3. Anyone he can ask who has an audience (classmates, creators, newsletter owners)?
4. Priority: the 1,000-paying number, or the strongest provable sprint outcome? And is this for revenue, learning, or portfolio?
5. Go/no-go on the five gates above.
