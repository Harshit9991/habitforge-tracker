# HabitForge - 10-Day Marketing Plan v1

HABITFORGE 10-DAY MARKETING PLAN v1 - ready for delivery. Built with the installed marketing-plan playbook, compressed from its 12-month format to his 10-day window. Budget and audience remain UNKNOWN and are marked as such; nothing executed. External claim sources: Product Hunt's own launch guide (https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch). Everything predictive is a labeled scenario, not a forecast.

=== 1. WHAT THIS PLAN OPTIMIZES FOR ===
Maximum evidence about who pays and why, inside 10 days and 5 total hours of Harshit's time - while taking a real shot at paying users. The 1,000 paying+active target is kept as the north condition, with the reach it honestly requires shown below rather than promised.

=== 2. ICP (TEST HYPOTHESIS - NOT VALIDATED) ===
Placement-focused students and freshers in India starting a defined 60-90 day preparation push, who have abandoned a tracker or plan before.
- Problem in their words (hypothesis): "I make a prep plan, lose consistency, and don't know where I'm slipping."
- Trigger: placements approaching, a bad mock, restarting prep.
- Paid-value hypothesis: they pay for patterns/insight on their prep habits, not another checkbox app. Untested - first conversations exist to test exactly this.
- Do not target: "everyone who wants better habits."

=== 3. FUNNEL SCENARIOS (assumptions, not benchmarks; product has zero measurement today) ===
- At an assumed 2% visitor-to-paid: 1,000 buyers needs ~50,000 visitors. At 5%: ~20,000.
- "Paying AND using": define "use" as at least one habit check-in after payment; return usage tracked separately. If some buyers never check in, the buyer count needed is higher than 1,000.
- Conditions that make 1,000 reachable: a large reachable audience (his groups/followers/partners - currently unknown) OR budget for paid reach (unknown) OR a viral hit (unplannable). Zero-spend + unknown audience means the plan aims to maximize provable paying+active users, and measures what the actual ceiling is.

=== 4. PRODUCT GATES (before recruiting paying users - need his go) ===
1. Fix the known paid-access control weakness.
2. Verify cross-device sync and per-user data isolation.
3. Add purchase + activation measurement (a checkout or a session recording alone proves neither payment nor use).
4. Show the tax-inclusive price (page says Rs 149, checkout charged Rs 175.82).
5. Decide guest mode vs forced Google sign-in (sign-in friction cuts activation).

=== 5. CHANNELS (ranked for his constraints) ===
1. Warm direct outreach - people and groups he already belongs to, in his voice, with admin permission where required. Doubles as customer research.
2. Focused study/productivity communities - a few, rules checked first, story-led posts ("built this, roast it"), real replies.
3. One short demo video on his own socials - one use case: track prep habits, see what's slipping. Reused in outreach and community posts.
4. Product Hunt as secondary - per PH's own guide there is no golden weekday, weekend launches suit side projects, start 12:01am PT (~12:31pm IST). Not the main bet for an India-first placement ICP.
Held: SEO (wrong window), influencers (too slow), ads (only after purchase/activation measurement exists).

=== 6. 10-DAY ROADMAP (his 30 min/day vs Instinct prep) ===
Day 1: His go on the 5 gates. He answers the two open questions (reachable audience size, budget). Instinct: gate fixes + measurement once approved.
Day 2: Instinct drafts warm-outreach messages + community posts; he reviews (10 min).
Day 3: He sends first warm batch (20-30 min of his day); Instinct cuts the demo video.
Day 4-5: He sends/replies; posts in 1-2 permitted communities. First paying users expected to come from here IF warm reach exists.
Day 6: Checkpoint 1 - apply test/stop/scale (below). He approves video; posts it.
Day 7-8: Double down on whatever produced real signups; optional Product Hunt prep if he wants it (his 30 min goes to assets review).
Day 9: Optional PH launch or second community angle; he spends his time replying to humans.
Day 10: Numbers review: visitors, signups, buyers, buyers-with-check-in. Decide whether the sprint proved the ICP and what a realistic path to 1,000 looks like from measured data.

=== 7. TEST / STOP / SCALE ===
- TEST: every channel gets one real, properly executed attempt (not a drive-by link drop).
- STOP: a channel that produces zero signups after one real attempt gets no more of his time this sprint.
- SCALE: whatever produces the first 10 paying users gets the remaining days' effort and the next iteration of message/video.
- Kill criterion for the ICP hypothesis: if warm placement-focused users try it free and none pay or check in, the hypothesis fails - that is a finding, not a wasted sprint.

=== 8. MEASUREMENT ===
- Sprint north star: paying users with at least one post-payment check-in.
- Leading indicators: visitors by source, signups, purchases, day-after check-ins.
- Cadence: Instinct compiles numbers daily; he reads a 2-line summary, replies where needed.

=== 9. OPEN DECISIONS (his answers change the plan) ===
1. Reachable audience now: groups, followers, potential introducers - roughly how many people?
2. Budget: any, or strictly zero-spend? (Plan above assumes zero-spend.)
3. Priority if they conflict: the 1,000 number vs a provable sprint outcome.
4. Go/no-go on the 5 product gates (Day 1 depends on this).
5. What this is for: revenue, learning, or portfolio - changes what "success" means on day 10.

Drafting work (messages, posts, video script, PH listing) is ready to start on his word. No sends, spend, or site changes without his approval.
