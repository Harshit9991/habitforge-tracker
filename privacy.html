HabitForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

HabitForge is a student habit tracker built as a course project. This page explains what it does with your information.

What we collect

When you choose "Sign in with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with HabitForge. We use them only to sign you in and show your name in the app. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

Your habit data

The habits and logs you enter are stored in your HabitForge account (hosted on Supabase) and sync across your signed-in devices. Only your signed-in account can read or change them. Entries saved before you sign in copy from your browser into your account on first login. Settings has two reset options: Restart Campaign clears your daily logs and keeps your habits; Erase All Data resets your habits and logs to a fresh start.

How we use it

Only to run the app for you. We do not sell, share or use your information for advertising.

Analytics

On the sign-in, plans and privacy pages we use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use those pages (behavioural metrics, heatmaps and session replay) so we can improve the product. Clarity sets cookies and similar identifiers only after you choose Accept on the analytics notice; if you decline, it collects only limited cookieless page-view data. These pages never show your habit data, and Clarity never receives your habits or logs. For how Microsoft collects and uses data, visit the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Third parties

Sign-in is handled by Google Identity Services. The app is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

Your choices

You can stop using HabitForge at any time. You can remove its access from your Google Account under Security, then Third-party apps and services.
